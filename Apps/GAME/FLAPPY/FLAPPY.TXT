^YFlappy^0 for MZ-800
(c) dB-Soft 1984

Logic game - move blue
ball to blue wall.

^WRIGHT^0 ....... right
^WUP^0 .......... up
^WLEFT^0 ........ left
^WDOWN^0 ........ down
^WSpace^0 ....... shoot mushroom
^WEnter^0 ....... solve scene
    (only first 8 scenes)
^WEsc^0 ......... quit
^WAlt+Tab^0 .... screenshot
