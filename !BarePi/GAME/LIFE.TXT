Cell life simulator
^WLeft^0 ....... left
^WRight^0 ...... right
^WUp^0 ......... up
^WDown^0 ....... down
^W[A]^0 ........ flip state
^W[B]^0 ........ run life
^W[X]^0 ........ next slot
^W[Y]^0 ........ quit
^WAlt+[X]^0 .... screenshot
Cell rules:
^W3 cells^0 .... born new cell
^W2 cells^0 .... stable state
^Wother^0 ...... dead
