^YFlappy^0 for MZ-800
(c) dB-Soft 1984

Logic game - move blue
ball to blue wall.

^WRight^0 ....... right
^WUp^0 .......... up
^WLeft^0 ........ left
^WDown^0 ........ down
^W[A]^0 ......... shoot mushroom
^W[B]^0 ......... solve scene
    (only first 8 scenes)
^W[Y]^0 ......... quit
^WAlt+[X]^0 ..... screenshot
